# CEO Planner: Product Synopsis

## The Problem
For many online entrepreneurs, coaches, and creators, the hardest part of growing a business isn't a lack of ideas or a poor work ethic—it's **Decision Fatigue**. Founders often wake up with a hundred conflicting ideas (marketing, sales, content creation, client delivery) and end up doing "busy work" that feels productive but doesn't generate revenue. They suffer from motion without momentum. Furthermore, tracking revenue, leads, and conversion rates is often scattered across messy spreadsheets, making it impossible to diagnose why the business isn't growing.

## The Solution
**The CEO Planner** is an intelligent business operating system designed specifically for solo-founders and female entrepreneurs. It replaces endless to-do lists and fragmented spreadsheets with a single "Command Center." The app forces users to define a 90-day vision, breaks it down into weekly strategy buckets (Visibility, Offers, Follow-ups), and extracts the "Daily 3" highest-leverage tasks. Paired with a built-in Executive AI Coach and 1-tap revenue tracking, it transforms founders from overwhelmed workers into focused CEOs.

---

## Target Audience

### Primary Demographic
- **Who:** Female entrepreneurs, online coaches, consultants, and course creators.
- **Stage:** Early-to-growth stage ($3k - $20k/month revenue).
- **Pain Points:** 
  - Overwhelmed by daily tasks and lacking strategic clarity.
  - Doing lots of "marketing" but struggling to track actual sales conversion.
  - Relying on messy, outdated Google Sheets to track leads.
  - Needs accountability but cannot yet afford a high-ticket business coach or Fractional CMO.

### Psychographics
- Values aesthetics, simplicity, and intuitive design (anti-clunky corporate software).
- Desires a balanced life (working fewer hours but with higher impact).
- Motivated by streaks, visual progress, and clear daily check-ins.

---

## Core Benefits

1. **Absolute Clarity:** Waking up every day knowing exactly what three things need to be done to drive revenue. No more guessing.
2. **Reclaimed Time:** By focusing only on the "Daily 3," users can cut out 10+ hours of busy work per week.
3. **Data-Driven Confidence:** Replacing gut-feelings with hard numbers. Instantly see if the bottleneck is lead flow (marketing) or close rate (sales).
4. **Accessible Mentorship:** The Executive AI Coach acts as an objective, 24/7 Board of Directors, providing brutally honest strategic advice based on real data, not generic platitudes.
5. **Momentum Building:** Gamified "streaks" and weekly progress tracking create psychological momentum that keeps solo-founders motivated.

## Key Features

### 1. The Execution Engine (Planning)
- **90-Day Vision Board:** Set a macro goal, a financial target, and the top 3 priorities for the quarter.
- **Weekly Strategy Filter:** A dedicated Monday workflow to dump tasks and categorize them into Visibility, Revenue Actions, or Follow-ups.
- **The Daily 3:** Automatically isolates the three most important actions for the day on a clean, distraction-free dashboard.

### 2. The Analytics Command Center (Revenue)
- **1-Tap Revenue Logging:** Log a new lead, booked call, or closed sale directly from the dashboard in seconds.
- **Automated Pipeline Metrics:** Instantly calculates Call Booking Rate, Close Rate, and overall Pipeline Conversion.
- **Source Attribution:** Visual pie charts showing exactly which platforms (Instagram, SEO, Referrals) and which offers are generating the most cash flow.
- **CSV Data Export:** One-click download of all financial logs for accounting or team hand-offs.

### 3. The Executive AI Coach (Intelligence)
- **Dynamic Weekly Insights:** Synthesizes the user's Friday Review and compares it to their revenue data to provide a "Focus Score."
- **Executive Strategic Briefing:** A 1-click feature that generates a comprehensive, downloadable health report of the business funnel, identifying precise bottlenecks (e.g., "High lead volume but low close rate—fix your sales script").
- **On-Demand Consultation:** A chat interface to bounce ideas off the AI, fully contextualized with the user's 90-day goals and current revenue metrics.

### 4. Behavioral Reinforcement
- **Friday Review:** A structured end-of-week reflection to log wins, assess bottlenecks, and maintain the CEO mindset.
- **Momentum Streaks:** Visual indicators for completing weekly planning and reviews.
